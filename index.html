02 — Synopsis
The Story

Every winter, millions of skiers and snowboarders go to the popular resorts of Aspen, Vail, and Park City, chasing the vast and challenging terrain of the Western Mountains. Tucked away in the hills of Central New York sits a small ski mountain called Labrador Mountain.

While the big mountains have thousands of employees who come from the states and countries abroad, local spots like Labrador rely on a different breed of employees: the volunteers. On Guard follows these volunteer ski patrollers, like Phil, to show what it actually takes to keep the slopes safe. During the working hours, these patrollers live normal lives; some are teachers, car salesmen and office workers, but when they clock out of work, they clock in as first responders on the mountain. Rather than for a paycheck, these regular people spend the cold weekends in a red jacket for the love of the mountain.

This film is not just about the work, but rather the comradery of the group that keeps the hill running. As the mountain opens and closes each day, all runs depend on decisions that most people never think about. The freedom on the slopes is only possible because someone like Phil is always watching.
