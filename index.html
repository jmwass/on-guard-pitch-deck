Director's Statement

I have been a skier my entire life. The mountain is not just a hobby for me, it is where I grew up. When I started noticing the patrol, I realized I had been taking them for granted my entire life. That felt like a story worth telling.

But this film is not just for skiers. On Guard is a story about ordinary people who show up every weekend for complete strangers. No paycheck, no recognition. Anyone can connect with that.

Synopsis
The
Story

Every winter, millions chase the terrain of Aspen, Vail, and Park City. Tucked away in the hills of Central New York sits Labrador Mountain.

On Guard follows the volunteers who keep it running. Teachers, car salesmen, office workers by week. First responders on the mountain by weekend.

"The freedom on the slopes is only possible because someone like Phil is always watching."
